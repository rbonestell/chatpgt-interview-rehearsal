# Interview Rehearsal privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Interview Rehearsal is a desktop-only plugin published by Bobby Bonestell. It provides interview instructions and a local PDF-generation script, using ChatGPT's available features. It has no MCP server, publisher-operated server, external API connection, or analytics code.

## Interview data

The publisher does not receive, collect, store, sell, or share your interview conversations, resumes, job descriptions, uploaded files, or voice recordings through this plugin. The plugin uses the information you choose to provide in your ChatGPT conversation to tailor questions and feedback. There is no separate publisher database or retention period for that interview data.

ChatGPT's processing, storage, sharing, retention, model-training settings, and deletion controls are governed by [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and the terms and settings applicable to your account or workspace. OpenAI's policy links to regional versions where applicable; business offerings may be governed by customer agreements. This plugin does not change those practices or controls. Use ChatGPT's account and workspace controls for requests concerning data held by OpenAI; the publisher cannot access or delete it.

When you request research or provide links, ChatGPT may use its browsing tools to contact websites. Those services have their own data practices. The plugin instructs ChatGPT not to include resume text or personal identifiers in company-research queries. Share only information you are authorized to disclose and redact unnecessary personal or confidential details.

## Local prep briefs

When you request a PDF, the assistant prepares structured study notes from your conversation and supplies them to the bundled local script. The script reads those notes and local artwork/fonts and writes a PDF to the selected local destination. It makes no network requests and does not send the notes or PDF to the publisher. Its PDF metadata uses a generic title and publisher name, rather than your name or resume details.

The saved PDF and any local input file remain until you or your host remove them; the plugin has no automatic retention or deletion schedule. The assistant is instructed to minimize personal details and remove temporary input files it creates after use. Keep or delete documents you intentionally save through your normal file controls. A PDF shared or attached through ChatGPT is also subject to the host's data practices described above. Installing the PDF runtime may download Python packages from a package registry; document generation itself runs locally.

## Support

If you voluntarily post a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/rbonestell/chatpgt-interview-rehearsal/issues), the publisher and other people with access to the repository can see your GitHub identity and the content you post. That information is used to address your report and is hosted by GitHub under its policies and controls. Do not post resumes, private conversations, credentials, or other sensitive information. Public issues can remain visible after resolution; use GitHub's editing and deletion controls for content you submit.

For questions about this policy, open an issue without including sensitive personal information.
